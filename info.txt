name = McPuppet Inventory System (Alpha)
author = AgeOfAlgorithms and Claude
description = ALPHA: early and in active development, it may change drastically. A real inventory on any map, single player and multiplayer: you start with nothing, pick things up (E), carry them in a grid, put tools on hotkeys 1-9, wear clothing and armour from clothing packs, buy and sell in the store with gold coins, and drop anything back into the world. Every weapon is a real object that can lie in the world and be picked back up, ammo is counted, bare hands punch, and you drop everything when you die. I opens the inventory.
